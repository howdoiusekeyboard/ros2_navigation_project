# Dr. Sujala Deepak Shetty

## Snapshot
- Associate Professor and Head, Department of Computer Science & Information Systems, BITS Pilani Dubai Campus
- Expertise: Artificial Intelligence, Big Data, Deep Neural Networks, Explainable AI, IoT, NLP, Recommender Systems, Security
- Faculty advisor for ACM and ACM-W; launched the first ACM-W chapter in the U.A.E. (2019) and led ACM chapter awards in 2020 & 2024

## Overview
Dr. Sujala D. Shetty has been with BITS Pilani Dubai Campus since September 2002, combining deep experience in teaching, research, and administration across undergraduate, postgraduate, and distance programs. Her work spans Big Data, AI, IoT, Web Services, NLP, and Cloud Computing.

## Education
- B.E., Bangalore Institute of Technology, 1994
- M.E., Manipal Institute of Technology, 2004
- Ph.D., BITS Pilani (Pilani Campus), 2010
